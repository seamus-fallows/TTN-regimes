\subsection{Locality of later escapes}\label{app:exp-locality}
Settings of \cref{sec:s2s-locality,sec:s2s-failure}. Experiment folder \path{experiments/locality/}
(README; one YAML configuration per sweep in \path{configs/}, written by \path{make_configs.py});
findings, and the changes made after two review rounds, in \path{notes/locality.md}, sections 7--10;
reviews in \path{notes/reviews/locality_round1.md} and
\path{notes/reviews/locality_round2_verification.md}.

\paragraph{Problems.}
Whitened Gaussian inputs, exact population loss, double precision, the library model
(\path{ttn.dynamics.Problem}). Pattern teachers (\path{ttn.teachers.pattern_teacher}) with $d=2$ and
random orthonormal site bases, teacher seed 1000, from a stream independent of the initialization
seeds. Two-component teachers have patterns $0^\Ns$ and the indicator of $D$, weights $(1,0.5)$, also
$(1,0.25)$, $(1,0.75)$ and $(2,1)$, on 60 (tree, $D$) pairs. Multi-component teachers (patterns as
site values, weights in brackets): TA $0000$ ($1$), $1100$ ($0.6$), $1110$ ($0.35$); TB as TA plus
$1111$ ($0.2$); N4 $0000$ ($1$), $1100$ ($0.6$), $1111$ ($0.4$); B4 $0000$ ($1$), $0110$ ($0.3$), $1001$
($0.6$); E8 $0^8$ ($1$), $0^31^20^3$ ($0.4$), $010^41\,0$ ($0.7$); TC $0^8$ ($1$), $1^20^6$ ($0.6$),
$1^40^4$ ($0.35$), $1^8$ ($0.2$); TD $0^8$ ($1$), $0^31^20^3$ ($0.6$), $1^20^6$ ($0.35$). Bond dimension
$\chi=2$, except $\chi=3$ for B4, N4 and E8, and $\chi\in\{4,8\}$, $d=4$ in the robustness sweep. Trees:
MPS4, MPS6, MPS8 ($\Kc=4,6,8$), binary4, binary8 ($\Kc=3,7$), HT4, HT8 ($\Kc=7,15$), star3 ($\Kc=4$: a
root without sites and three children carrying sites $\{0,1\}$, $\{2,3\}$, $\{4,5\}$) and midroot6
($\Kc=6$: the MPS6 chain rooted at the core of site 3).

\paragraph{Protocols and dynamics.}
\emph{Small}: fan-in initialization \eqref{eq:init}. \emph{Saddle}: the exact balanced saddle $\tw1\U1$
(\path{ttn.network.aligned_init}) plus a fan-in perturbation of per-core scale $\isc$.
\emph{Balanced}: the fan-in $\Wz$ re-gauged so that $\Q e=0$ on every bond. Gradient flow by SciPy's
LSODA (\path{solve_ivp}) with the exact Hessian as Jacobian, relative and absolute tolerances $10^{-9}$
and $10^{-14}$, instead of the default integrator of \cref{sec:setup-dynamics}; Radau as fallback
(fewer than $1\%$ of runs). Event times are located by root finding on the dense output; event $r$ is
the last upward crossing of $\ov r=\frac12$, and a run with an overlap that crosses $\frac12$ more than
once is a \emph{rotation}. Budgets: $t_{\max}=10^{14}$ and a wall-clock limit of $1500$\,s per run
($900$\,s for the $k\ge6$ rows of the main sweep, $2400$\,s in the $k\ge6$ extension and the stall
diagnosis). A run is \emph{stalled} when, after an overlap has passed $\frac12$, every gradient entry stays
below $10^{-12}$ for more than 5,000 consecutive evaluations (300,000 in the stall diagnosis, which
also keeps the trajectory). The Hessian is computed by automatic differentiation at the plateau
midpoint (minimum gradient norm between consecutive events), raw and with the gauge directions
projected out (\path{ttn.diagnostics.project_out_gauge}, relative cutoff $10^{-3}$). An earlier cutoff,
$10^{-10}$, kept the generators of nearly unused bond channels, whose normalized direction overlaps the
escape route, and biased the $k=2$ values to $-0.44$ to $-0.46$ instead of $-0.5000$; all Hessians were
recomputed (\path{hess_recompute.py}). The fixed projection is still biased on the short
small-initialization $k=2$ plateaus at $\isc\ge10^{-3}$ ($-0.411$ and $-0.502$ against raw $-0.437$ and
$-0.581$), so raw values are quoted there; raw and projected $\isc$-exponents of $\lambda_{\min}$ differ by
up to $0.11$.

\paragraph{Analysis.}
Run classes: greedy, other order, rotation, stalled, timeout, incomplete, error. Fits as in
\cref{sec:s2s-locality} (per seed, at least three $\isc$; rotations excluded; the log law is fitted on
the residuals of $\ln\Delta t_2$, like the power law). Several components: plateaus detected by
\path{ttn.diagnostics.find_plateaus} on $\Loss(t)$ (linear time, separation ratio above $3$), matched to
a predicted level within $5\%$; laws of levels too short to detect from the time spent within $2\%$ of
the level. Bond events: effective ranks just after the event ($\ov2=0.9$) against the plateau midpoint,
threshold $0.05$ times the teacher's singular value ($0.03$, $0.1$ as sensitivity); rank profiles of
several components in the middle of the time spent within $2\%$ of each predicted level. Growth
exponent: least squares of $\ln\dot{\ov2}$ against $\ln\ov2$ over $\ov2\in[10^{-4},10^{-2}]$.

\paragraph{Accuracy.}
The conservation drift $\max_e\normmax{\Q e(T)-\Q e(0)}$ is at most $10^{-6}$ in every run ($99.9\%$
below $3.5\cdot10^{-8}$, median $1.2\cdot10^{-9}$), against Gram entries of order one after learning.
Relative to the initial imbalance $\abs{\Q e(0)}\sim\isc^2$ it is at most $1.6\cdot10^{-4}$ in every run
with $k\ge4$, where the imbalance matters (\cref{sec:s2s-failure}), but up to $0.5$ for $k\le3$ at
$\isc\le10^{-3}$, where $\isc^2$ is below the absolute tolerance; those runs never stall, and their gaps
agree with an independent integration to $10^{-7}$--$10^{-9}$. LSODA (tolerances $10^{-9}$ and
$10^{-10}$), Radau and RK45 agree on a $k=6$ gap to $3\cdot10^{-8}$. A reviewer's independent code (dense
contraction, automatic differentiation, Radau at relative tolerance $10^{-10}$) reproduced the gaps of
11 rows to $10^{-7}$--$10^{-9}$, the gauge-projected $\lambda_{\min}$ to $7\cdot10^{-11}$ and the Steiner
counts of all 60 (tree, $D$) pairs.

\paragraph{Excluded runs.}
One \texttt{lambda2\_K} run (MPS8, seed 7, $\isc=10^{-4}$) was lost to the runner's start-up check,
which compared two implementations of the velocity at the exact saddle without an absolute floor.
Six slow runs did not finish within their time limit; they belong to rows reported as not measured
($k=8$) or as spanning $0.6$ decades (HT8, $k=7$). Round-1 tables (rows on $\isc$ grids outside the
committed configurations, rotation runs pooled, Hessians with the biased projection, a bond-rank
statistic taken at the end of the run) are superseded. The raw run records and trajectories (every
accepted integration step of every run, 8\,GB) are not in the repository; \cref{app:reproducibility}
says how to regenerate them.

\paragraph{Local pilot.}
The pilot in \path{experiments/pilot_h2_locality/} (README; code \path{h2sim.py},
\path{run_sweep.py} and \path{analyze.py}) is an independent implementation of the same model, used as
a cross-check in \cref{sec:s2s-locality}. $d=\chi=2$; MPS4, binary4, MPS6, binary8; teacher seed 1000, weights $(1,0.5)$;
$D=\{0,1\}$, $\{0,\Ns-1\}$, all sites, $\{0,2\}$, $\{0,3\}$, $\{0,4\}$; fan-in initialization with seeds
$0$--$7$ ($\Ns=4$; $\isc\in[10^{-3},0.1]$ on MPS4, $[2\cdot10^{-4},0.05]$ on binary4) and $0$--$5$ (MPS6,
$\isc\in[0.05,0.3]$; binary8, $[0.07,0.3]$): 784 runs, 19 of them rerun with a 100 times longer horizon,
plus 340 runs from the saddle (seeds $0$--$3$, five $\isc$). Gradient flow by LSODA with the exact
Hessian (relative tolerance $10^{-10}$, absolute $10^{-13}\isc$); events are first crossings of
$\ov r=\frac12$, located by root finding; $t_1$ and $t_2$ agree with LSODA at $10^{-12}$, Radau, BDF,
DOP853 and RK45 to $1.8\cdot10^{-7}$ relative (47 comparisons). A run \emph{collapses} when, at
$t\ge30\,t_1$, the largest second bond singular value is below $10^{-9}$ of its value after the first
escape and still falling, or at roundoff; horizons $10^5\isc^{-(\Kc-2)}$ ($\Ns=4$) and
$10^4\isc^{-(\Kc-2)}$. Fits per seed, mean $\pm$ s.d.\ over seeds; gap fits for $k\ge3$ use runs with a
distinct intermediate plateau. A first sweep, made before the teacher and initialization streams
were separated, was discarded.

\subsection{Optimizers used in practice}\label{app:exp-optimizers}
Settings of \cref{sec:other-optimizers}. Experiment folder \path{experiments/optimizers/} (one YAML
configuration per sweep in \path{configs/}); findings and claims withdrawn after review in
\path{notes/optimizers.md}, sections 2, 3 and 5.

\paragraph{Problems.}
Whitened Gaussian inputs, exact population loss (empirical loss in the finite-sample sweep), double
precision. Teacher seed 100; initialization seeds 0--5, from a stream independent of the teacher's.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{binary4}: binary tree, $\Ns=4$ ($\Kc=3$), $d=3$, $\chi=4$; odeco teacher with weights
    $(3,2,1)$ and Haar-random site bases.
  \item \emph{binary8}, \emph{MPS8}: $\Ns=8$ ($\Kc=7$ and $8$), the same odeco teacher, $d=3$, $\chi=4$.
  \item \emph{E8} on binary8 and MPS8: pattern teacher with $d=2$, $\chi=3$ and patterns
    $\pi_0=(1,\dots,1)$ (weight $1$), $\pi_A$ equal to $2$ at sites 4 and 5 (weight $0.4$) and
    $\pi_B$ equal to $2$ at sites 2 and 7 (weight $0.7$), $1$ elsewhere. The cores that carry the
    differing sites span $k=2$ ($\pi_A$) and $k=6$ ($\pi_B$) cores on the MPS and $k=5$ for both on
    the binary tree.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Initializations.}
Fan-in \eqref{eq:init} with per-core scale $\isc$; \emph{balanced}: the same $\Wz$ in the gauge with
$\Q e=0$ on every edge. All deep-tree baselines use the balanced version, because fan-in gradient flow
converges by $t=2\cdot10^6$ in none of the 12 MPS8 odeco runs (the conservation-law trap of
\cref{prop:A-trap}, verified at the route ends in 11 of the 12 stalls), against 10 of 12 (binary8
odeco), 11 of 12 (binary8 E8) and 11 of 12 (MPS8 E8) fan-in runs. Methods whose iterates depend on the
gauge were run from both versions; re-gauged GD, Riemannian GD, ALS and DMRG depend only on $\Wz$
and were run from the balanced one.

\paragraph{Dynamics.}
All methods stop at $\Loss<10^{-6}\Loss_0$ (finite samples: empirical loss below $10^{-8}$ times its
initial value) or at their budget.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{Gradient flow}: SciPy LSODA with relative and absolute tolerances $10^{-9}$ and
    $10^{-14}$ and the exact Hessian as Jacobian (Radau as fallback), instead of the default
    integrator of \cref{sec:setup-dynamics}; the crossings $\ov r\in\{0.1,0.5,0.9\}$ are located by
    root finding, so event times are exact. Budgets: $t_{\max}=8/\isc$ (binary4), $2\cdot10^6$
    (deep trees), $4\cdot10^6$ (binary8 scan), $10^6$ (finite samples).
  \item \emph{GD and heavy ball} (PyTorch): $\lr=0.02$ and $0.002$ with $\beta=0.9$ on binary4
    ($0.13\,\lr_c$ for both, counting heavy ball's effective step $\lr/(1-\beta)$); $\lr=0.01$ and
    $0.001$ on the deep trees ($0.23$--$0.27\,\lr_c$), at $\isc=0.8$ only, because gradient-flow
    plateaus at $\isc=0.3$ last $10^4$--$10^6$ time units. Budgets $8/\isc$ (binary4) and $2\cdot10^4$
    (deep) gradient-flow time units, at most $5\cdot10^5$ steps.
  \item \emph{Adam} (PyTorch, bias-corrected): $\beta_1=0.9$, $\beta_2=0.999$ (also $0.99$ and $0.9$
    at $\lr=10^{-3}$ on binary4), $\epsilon=10^{-8}$ ($10^{-30}$ in the $\epsilon$ sweep),
    $\lr\in\{10^{-4},3\cdot10^{-4},10^{-3},10^{-2}\}$ on binary4 and $\{10^{-3},10^{-2}\}$ on the deep
    trees. \emph{RMSProp}: $\alpha=0.99$, $\epsilon=10^{-8}$, $\lr\in\{10^{-4},10^{-3}\}$. Budgets
    $10^5$--$3\cdot10^5$ (binary4), $3\cdot10^5$ (deep), $5\cdot10^5$ (binary8 scan) and $2\cdot10^5$
    ($\epsilon$ sweep) steps.
  \item \emph{Re-gauged GD}: $\lr=0.02$ (binary4) and $0.01$ (deep); QR sweeps from the leaves to
    the root bring the cores to the root-centred canonical form every 1 or 50 steps; $5\cdot10^4$,
    $10^5$ and $2\cdot10^5$ (binary8 scan) steps.
  \item \emph{Riemannian GD}: $\lr\in\{0.1,1\}$; tangent projection by least squares with the dense
    Jacobian of $\W(\params)$ (relative cutoff $10^{-12}$), so only small $d^\Ns$ are feasible;
    leaves-to-root HSVD retraction; 2000 steps.
  \item \emph{Gram-preconditioned GD}: $\lr\in\{0.01,0.1,0.5\}$; each Kronecker factor of a core's
    Hessian block is damped by $10^{-3}$ times its mean eigenvalue (scale-free) or, at $\lr=0.1$, by
    an absolute floor $10^{-4}$ or $10^{-2}$; no divergence guard, because the first step overshoots
    by up to $10^{34}\Loss_0$ before the loss returns; $5\cdot10^4$ (binary4; $2\cdot10^5$ with a
    floor) and $10^5$ (deep) steps.
  \item \emph{ALS} (one site) and \emph{DMRG} (two sites, then SVD truncation to at most $\chi$
    values with relative cutoff $10^{-12}$): exact least squares; the orthogonality centre moves by QR
    along a depth-first tour of the tree; at most 50 sweeps.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Events and classification.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Event $r$ is the last upward crossing of $\ov r=\frac12$ after which $\ov r$ stays at least
    $\frac12$; transient crossings, which the first-crossing rule of \cref{sec:setup-diagnostics}
    would count, are not events. Width: final rise from $0.1$ to $0.9$. Saddle-to-saddle: every
    separation ratio $\sep i>3$ (\cref{tab:regimes}).
  \item Discrete methods record loss and overlaps at every step for the first 2000 steps, then every
    $\max(1,n_{\rm steps}/(5\cdot10^4))$ steps. A first escape or gap shorter than two recording
    intervals is unresolved and excluded from fits (and counted in the result tables). Two components
    that cross $\frac12$ in the same step or micro-step have no resolved order. For Gram-preconditioned
    GD, events are defined only after the loss has returned below $\Loss_0$; a component already above
    $\frac12$ at that point has no resolved event.
  \item Exponents: per seed, a least-squares fit of $\ln T$ against $\ln\isc$ over the $\isc$ at which
    the seed follows its most common learning order (for plateau durations, not for times to reach a
    loss level); mean $\pm$ standard error over seeds. A logarithmic law fitted in the same space
    serves as comparison (gradient flow on binary4: root-mean-square residual of $\ln T$ $0.005$ for
    the power law, $0.62$ for the logarithmic law).
  \item Orders are compared with gradient flow only where both are resolved, for the same problem,
    $\isc$ and seed. Re-gauged GD, Riemannian GD, ALS and DMRG give one trajectory per seed at every
    $\isc$, so their orders are counted over the six independent seeds (at the smallest $\isc$).
  \item \emph{$\epsilon$ test} \eqref{eq:adam-eps}: $T_{\rm GF}(\isc)$ is the seed's exact
    gradient-flow first-escape time at $\isc=0.3$ (\texttt{deep} sweep, same balanced initial point
    up to scale) times $(0.3/\isc)^6$, and $n_{\rm sign}$ the median over $\isc$ of the same seed's
    escape with $\epsilon=10^{-30}$ at the same $\lr$. Runs without an escape within $2\cdot10^5$ steps
    enter only through the check that the prediction exceeds the budget.
  \item Convergence for cost and time-to-loss tables: $\Loss<10^{-3}\Loss_0$. Runs that hit their budget
    are reported as budget-limited, never as stalls. RMSProp hovers above $10^{-6}\Loss_0$ on the deep
    trees within $3\cdot10^5$ steps; its events are used, its convergence flags are not.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Excluded runs.}
Runs replaced after review round 1 are kept in \path{superseded/} subfolders of the raw data and
ignored by the analysis: gradient-flow runs whose event times were interpolated on an output grid
(errors up to $1.7\%$), Gram-preconditioned runs stopped by a $10^6\Loss_0$ loss guard after one step,
binary8 Adam runs recorded only every 10 steps, and the first finite-sample sweep
(\texttt{implicit\_bias\_b4}, 288 runs, one data draw per initialization). The pilots
(\texttt{pilot\_calib}, \texttt{pilot\_trap}) support no claim. Raw trajectories (one file per run,
2.2\,GB) are not in the repository; \cref{app:reproducibility} says how to regenerate them.
