\paragraph{Problems.}
Whitened Gaussian inputs, exact population loss, double precision. Odeco teachers
(\path{ttn.teachers.odeco_teacher}) with $\ncomp=3$, $d=4$ at every site and Haar-random site bases from
teacher seed 1000, a stream independent of the initialization seeds; weights $(3,2,1)$ (separated) and
$(1,0.85,0.7)$ (close). Trees binary4, MPS4, binary8, HT4 and MPS8 ($\Kc=3,4,7,7,8$); nominal bond
dimension $\chi=3$ or $6$, capped by \eqref{eq:bond-cap} (at $\chi=6$: MPS4 bonds $(4,6,4)$, MPS8 end bonds
and HT4 leaf bonds $4$, all other bonds $6$). Initializations: fan-in \eqref{eq:init} with seeds $0$--$9$;
\emph{balanced}, the same $\Wz$ re-gauged to $\Q e=0$ on every bond (\path{s2s.gauge_balance}); \emph{aligned},
the same per-core amplitude on every core and component (\path{ttn.network.aligned_init}). The
leading-order sweeps also use MPS, binary and HT trees with up to 16 sites (\cref{tab:exp-s2s}).

\paragraph{Dynamics.}
Gradient flow by SciPy's LSODA (\path{ttn.dynamics.integrate_gf}) with the exact Hessian as Jacobian,
instead of the default integrator of \cref{sec:setup-dynamics}: past the first escape the flow is stiff,
and an explicit method needs a number of steps $\propto\isc^{-(\Kc-2)}$ per plateau (Radau, tried on one
$\Kc=7$ run, did not finish in 25 minutes). Tolerances: relative $10^{-8}$ and absolute $10^{-11}\isc$
(sweeps \texttt{main}, \texttt{first}, \texttt{balanced}), or $10^{-6}$ and $10^{-9}\isc$ (\texttt{large},
\texttt{k78}, whose plateaus last up to $10^{7}$); on binary8 at $\chi=6$, $\isc=0.07$, the looser tolerances
move the event times by $7\cdot10^{-7}$ relative. Aligned runs: Dormand--Prince 5(4) and LSODA with
tolerances $10^{-10}$ and $10^{-14}$. Events are last upward crossings of $\ov r=\frac12$ and widths final
rises from $0.1$ to $0.9$ (\cref{sec:setup-diagnostics}), both refined by re-integrating the event window on
linear grids until the rise holds at least 40 samples (\path{s2s.zoom_rise}); no duration is read off the
logarithmic output grid. The plateau midpoint is the time of minimum parameter speed between two events;
the parameters there are saved, and the Hessian, raw and gauge-projected
(\path{ttn.diagnostics.project_out_gauge}), was recomputed from them after the library's projection fix
(\path{recompute_hessians.py}). Bond spectra are recorded on the output grid and, in all but the first 332
complete runs of \texttt{main}, in the event windows. The conservation drift (per integration chunk, as in
\eqref{eq:drift}) has median $9\cdot10^{-10}$ and exceeds $10^{-6}$ in 11 of 1,576 runs (at most
$3.3\cdot10^{-6}$; ten MPS8 runs and one binary8 run).

\paragraph{Leading-order flow.}
$T(Z)$ and $r_1$ (\cref{rem:A-recipe}) come from \path{s2s.correlation_flow_blowup}: the flow
$\dot\params=\nabla g$ from the initialization at $\isc=1$, by DOP853 with relative tolerance $10^{-10}$,
integrated in segments until the second-largest component amplitude $\abs{\ip{\W}{\U r}}$ is below $10^{-6}$
of the largest and no longer growing relative to it, with a cap at amplitude $10^{30}$; the remaining time
of a balanced mode from the stop is added. The 40 binary4 close-teacher jobs and 2 separated binary4 jobs
at $\chi=6$ stopped at the cap with the direction still converging; $T$ is converged regardless (the time
left beyond amplitude $A$ is $\propto A^{-(\Kc-2)/\Kc}$), and an independent integration of the spherical
flow agrees to $1.5\cdot10^{-11}$ (binary4) and $1.7\cdot10^{-10}$ (MPS4) relative on all 160 binary4 and
MPS4 jobs (verification review). Round-1 values of $T$, from a stop at amplitude $10^6$, were biased by up to
$2.2\cdot10^{-3}$ relative (binary4, close teacher) and are superseded.

\paragraph{Budgets and run classes.}
Runs stop at $\Loss\le10^{-9}\Loss(0)$; at $t_{\max}$, $10^3$ times the RMS-overlap escape time of the weakest
component; at a wall-clock limit ($300$\,s in \texttt{main}, $400$\,s in \texttt{balanced}, $2400$\,s in
\texttt{large} and \texttt{k78}, so the number of unresolved runs depends on the machine) or a step limit per
output chunk; and, except in the first ${\sim}310$ runs of \texttt{main}, when a component never learned has a
route-end slice below $10^{-8}\isc$ for a whole output chunk (a dead end, after which nothing is physics).
\texttt{main} also stopped when every dormant amplitude $\abs{\ip{\W}{\U r}}$ stayed below $10^{-15}$ for a
chunk; at $\Kc=7,8$ this was too strict (the amplitude is a product of $\Kc$ per-core amplitudes and can be
$10^{-15}$ while each of them is $10^{-3}$), so those runs are classed unresolved unless a slice is dead, and
the cells were rerun without it in \texttt{k78}. Classes (\path{analyze_main.py}): \emph{complete} (all three
components learned); \emph{trapped} (a component never learned has a route-end slice below $10^{-8}\isc$ after
the first escape); \emph{stalled} (the time budget ended with every such slice above it); \emph{unresolved}
(wall-clock limit or the amplitude stop). The classes do not depend on the tolerance: the 9 runs of the
tolerance check keep their class and order, and their event times change by at most $3.6\cdot10^{-6}$
relative. At $\Kc=7,8$ the trapped fractions are lower bounds, since some stalled runs end with slices still
decaying (\path{slice_trends.py}).

\paragraph{Fits.}
Exponents are fitted per seed, $\ln$ of the time against $\ln\isc$ with at least four values of $\isc$, on run
sets classified by the saddle sequence (\path{analyze_main.fit_sets}): $\tev1$ on the runs whose first
component is the seed's most frequent one; $\tev2-\tev1$ on the runs whose first two components are the
seed's most frequent pair, with $\sep1>3$; $\tev3-\tev2$ on complete runs in the seed's most frequent order
with $\sep1,\sep2>3$. The Hessian slopes use the same sets. Each cell comes from one sweep (\texttt{large} at
$\Kc=7,8$ with $\chi=6$, \texttt{main} otherwise); sweeps with different tolerances are never pooled. The cut
$\sep i>3$ matters only for binary4 with the close teacher, where it leaves some seeds with four or five
values of $\isc$ (about $0.8$ decade); without it, overlapping and wrong-order runs are mixed in and the
second gap gives $0.92\pm0.11$ at $\chi=3$ and $1.12\pm0.05$ at $\chi=6$ (verification review, E9).
First-escape exponents are also given with the offset $\delta_\Kc\tw{r_1}^{-(2-2/\Kc)}$ of
\eqref{eq:s2s-first} subtracted from $\tev1$.

\paragraph{Continued runs.}
\path{continue_stalled.py} integrates a stalled or unresolved run further from its saved final state, with the
escape time resolved like every event. Three samples: the 8 stalled \texttt{k78} runs with the shortest predicted
remaining plateau (3 seeds); a seeded random sample (\texttt{-{}-seed 1}) of 8 of the 74 \texttt{k78} runs (73
stalled, 1 trapped) whose estimate is at most 100 times the time already integrated, one of them also in the
first sample; and 8 MPS8 runs of \texttt{large}, spread over the ratio of estimate to elapsed time
($0.2$--$420$). The budget multiplier of the third sample was not recorded, and the README command selects a
different set (verification review, E13); its results are reported as measured.

\paragraph{Excluded and superseded runs.}
The scipy pilots made before the library existed, part of them before the teacher and initialization streams
were separated, support no claim. Superseded after review round 1: $T$ from the amplitude-$10^6$ stop (kept as
\texttt{T\_v1} in the raw records), gap fits on pooled run sets, Hessians with the earlier gauge projection, and
decay rates fitted into the floating-point floor (now fitted above $10^{-9}$ absolute). No run was dropped:
runs that do not complete are reported in their class. The raw records (3,417 files, about 220\,MB; local copy
\path{data/s2s_core/}) are not in the repository; \cref{app:reproducibility} says how to regenerate them.

\paragraph{Problems.}
Whitened Gaussian inputs, exact population loss, double precision, the library model
(\path{ttn.dynamics.Problem}). Pattern teachers (\path{ttn.teachers.pattern_teacher}) with $d=2$ and
random orthonormal site bases, teacher seed 1000, from a stream independent of the initialization
seeds. Two-component teachers have patterns $0^\Ns$ and the indicator of $D$, weights $(1,0.5)$, also
$(1,0.25)$, $(1,0.75)$ and $(2,1)$, on 60 (tree, $D$) pairs. Multi-component teachers (patterns as
site values, weights in brackets): TA $0000$ ($1$), $1100$ ($0.6$), $1110$ ($0.35$); TB as TA plus
$1111$ ($0.2$); N4 $0000$ ($1$), $1100$ ($0.6$), $1111$ ($0.4$); B4 $0000$ ($1$), $0110$ ($0.3$), $1001$
($0.6$); E8 $0^8$ ($1$), $0^31^20^3$ ($0.4$), $010^41\,0$ ($0.7$); TC $0^8$ ($1$), $1^20^6$ ($0.6$),
$1^40^4$ ($0.35$), $1^8$ ($0.2$); TD $0^8$ ($1$), $0^31^20^3$ ($0.6$), $1^20^6$ ($0.35$). Bond dimension
$\chi=2$, except $\chi=3$ for B4, N4 and E8, and $\chi\in\{4,8\}$, $d=4$ in the robustness sweep. Trees:
MPS4, MPS6, MPS8 ($\Kc=4,6,8$), binary4, binary8 ($\Kc=3,7$), HT4, HT8 ($\Kc=7,15$), star3 ($\Kc=4$: a
root without sites and three children carrying sites $\{0,1\}$, $\{2,3\}$, $\{4,5\}$) and midroot6
($\Kc=6$: the MPS6 chain rooted at the core of site 3).

\paragraph{Protocols and dynamics.}
\emph{Small}: fan-in initialization \eqref{eq:init}. \emph{Saddle}: the exact balanced saddle $\tw1\U1$
(\path{ttn.network.aligned_init}) plus a fan-in perturbation of per-core scale $\isc$.
\emph{Balanced}: the fan-in $\Wz$ re-gauged so that $\Q e=0$ on every bond. Gradient flow by SciPy's
LSODA (\path{solve_ivp}) with the exact Hessian as Jacobian, relative and absolute tolerances $10^{-9}$
and $10^{-14}$, instead of the default integrator of \cref{sec:setup-dynamics}; Radau as fallback
(fewer than $1\%$ of runs). Event times are located by root finding on the dense output; event $r$ is
the last upward crossing of $\ov r=\frac12$, and a run with an overlap that crosses $\frac12$ more than
once is a \emph{rotation}. Budgets: $t_{\max}=10^{14}$ and a wall-clock limit of $1500$\,s per run
($900$\,s for the $k\ge6$ rows of the main sweep, $2400$\,s in the $k\ge6$ extension and the stall
diagnosis). A run is \emph{stalled} when, after an overlap has passed $\frac12$, every gradient entry stays
below $10^{-12}$ for more than 5,000 consecutive evaluations (300,000 in the stall diagnosis, which
also keeps the trajectory). The Hessian is computed by automatic differentiation at the plateau
midpoint (minimum gradient norm between consecutive events), raw and with the gauge directions
projected out (\path{ttn.diagnostics.project_out_gauge}, relative cutoff $10^{-3}$). An earlier cutoff,
$10^{-10}$, kept the generators of nearly unused bond channels, whose normalized direction overlaps the
escape route, and biased the $k=2$ values to $-0.44$ to $-0.46$ instead of $-0.5000$; all Hessians were
recomputed (\path{hess_recompute.py}). The fixed projection is still biased on the short
small-initialization $k=2$ plateaus at $\isc\ge10^{-3}$ ($-0.411$ and $-0.502$ against raw $-0.437$ and
$-0.581$), so raw values are quoted there; raw and projected $\isc$-exponents of $\lambda_{\min}$ differ by
up to $0.11$.

\paragraph{Analysis.}
Run classes: greedy, other order, rotation, stalled, timeout, incomplete, error. Fits as in
\cref{sec:s2s-locality} (per seed, at least three $\isc$; rotations excluded; the log law is fitted on
the residuals of $\ln\Delta t_2$, like the power law). Several components: plateaus detected by
\path{ttn.diagnostics.find_plateaus} on $\Loss(t)$ (linear time, separation ratio above $3$), matched to
a predicted level within $5\%$; laws of levels too short to detect from the time spent within $2\%$ of
the level. Bond events: effective ranks just after the event ($\ov2=0.9$) against the plateau midpoint,
threshold $0.05$ times the teacher's singular value ($0.03$, $0.1$ as sensitivity); rank profiles of
several components in the middle of the time spent within $2\%$ of each predicted level. Growth
exponent: least squares of $\ln\dot{\ov2}$ against $\ln\ov2$ over $\ov2\in[10^{-4},10^{-2}]$.

\paragraph{Accuracy.}
The conservation drift $\max_e\normmax{\Q e(T)-\Q e(0)}$ is at most $10^{-6}$ in every run ($99.9\%$
below $3.5\cdot10^{-8}$, median $1.2\cdot10^{-9}$), against Gram entries of order one after learning.
Relative to the initial imbalance $\abs{\Q e(0)}\sim\isc^2$ it is at most $1.6\cdot10^{-4}$ in every run
with $k\ge4$, where the imbalance matters (\cref{sec:s2s-failure}), but up to $0.5$ for $k\le3$ at
$\isc\le10^{-3}$, where $\isc^2$ is below the absolute tolerance; those runs never stall, and their gaps
agree with an independent integration to $10^{-7}$--$10^{-9}$. LSODA (tolerances $10^{-9}$ and
$10^{-10}$), Radau and RK45 agree on a $k=6$ gap to $3\cdot10^{-8}$. A reviewer's independent code (dense
contraction, automatic differentiation, Radau at relative tolerance $10^{-10}$) reproduced the gaps of
11 rows to $10^{-7}$--$10^{-9}$, the gauge-projected $\lambda_{\min}$ to $7\cdot10^{-11}$ and the Steiner
counts of all 60 (tree, $D$) pairs.

\paragraph{Excluded runs.}
One \texttt{lambda2\_K} run (MPS8, seed 7, $\isc=10^{-4}$) was lost to the runner's start-up check,
which compared two implementations of the velocity at the exact saddle without an absolute floor.
Six slow runs did not finish within their time limit; they belong to rows reported as not measured
($k=8$) or as spanning $0.6$ decades (HT8, $k=7$). Round-1 tables (rows on $\isc$ grids outside the
committed configurations, rotation runs pooled, Hessians with the biased projection, a bond-rank
statistic taken at the end of the run) are superseded. The raw run records and trajectories (every
accepted integration step of every run, 8\,GB) are not in the repository; \cref{app:reproducibility}
says how to regenerate them.

\paragraph{Local pilot.}
The pilot in \path{experiments/pilot_h2_locality/} (README; code \path{h2sim.py},
\path{run_sweep.py} and \path{analyze.py}) is an independent implementation of the same model, used as
a cross-check in \cref{sec:s2s-locality}. $d=\chi=2$; MPS4, binary4, MPS6, binary8; teacher seed 1000, weights $(1,0.5)$;
$D=\{0,1\}$, $\{0,\Ns-1\}$, all sites, $\{0,2\}$, $\{0,3\}$, $\{0,4\}$; fan-in initialization with seeds
$0$--$7$ ($\Ns=4$; $\isc\in[10^{-3},0.1]$ on MPS4, $[2\cdot10^{-4},0.05]$ on binary4) and $0$--$5$ (MPS6,
$\isc\in[0.05,0.3]$; binary8, $[0.07,0.3]$): 784 runs, 19 of them rerun with a 100 times longer horizon,
plus 340 runs from the saddle (seeds $0$--$3$, five $\isc$). Gradient flow by LSODA with the exact
Hessian (relative tolerance $10^{-10}$, absolute $10^{-13}\isc$); events are first crossings of
$\ov r=\frac12$, located by root finding; $t_1$ and $t_2$ agree with LSODA at $10^{-12}$, Radau, BDF,

escape and still falling, or at roundoff; horizons $10^5\isc^{-(\Kc-2)}$ ($\Ns=4$) and
$10^4\isc^{-(\Kc-2)}$. Fits per seed, mean $\pm$ s.d.\ over seeds; gap fits for $k\ge3$ use runs with a
distinct intermediate plateau. A first sweep, made before the teacher and initialization streams
were separated, was discarded.

\subsection{Covariance and teacher experiments}\label{app:exp-data-teacher}
Settings of \cref{sec:covariance-teacher}. Experiment folder \path{experiments/data_teacher/} (README with
the run order; YAML configurations in \path{configs/}; the star and correlated-input sweeps are defined
in \path{r2_star.py} and \path{r2_corr.py}); findings in \path{notes/data_teacher.md}; reviews in
\path{notes/reviews/} (\path{data_teacher_round1.md}, \path{data_teacher_round2.md} and
\path{data_teacher_round3_verification.md}, whose errata are applied in \cref{sec:covariance-teacher});
theory in \cref{app:teachers,app:covariance}.

\paragraph{Problems.}
Exact population loss, double precision, fan-in initialization \eqref{eq:init}; teacher seeds from a
stream independent of the initialization seeds. Site covariances \path{ttn.losses.power_law_cov}$(d,\beta)$
(eigenvalues $\propto k^{-\beta}$, trace $d$), rotated by $\theta$ in the $(e_1,e_2)$ plane where misaligned.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{Aligned covariance} ($\Ns=4$, $d=\chi=4$): odeco teachers on covariance eigenvectors, T1 $(1,0.8)$
    and T2 $(1,0.6)$ with the smaller component on $e_1$, $\beta=0.25$; one-component teachers on $e_1$ or $e_4$
    with $\beta=0$--$1.5$ ($\Lambda=0.008$--$33$); three components $(1,0.7,0.5)$ for the exact-clock check. Variants
    from the same initialization: $(\Sig,\tw{})$, $(\Id,\tw{})$, $(\Id,\Lambda\tw{})$, $(\Id,\Lambda^{1/2}\tw{})$, and $(\Id,\Lambda^{1/2}\tw{})$

\paragraph{Events and classification.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Overlap events, in the GD, gradient-flow and SGD sweeps and the route test: the last upward
    crossing of $\ov r=\frac12$, with $\ov r\ge\frac12$ at the end of the record (\path{common.record_events}, or the
    integrator's event finder for the DOP853 references); first crossings and the number of crossings
    are kept to count transients; widths are final rises (the first crossing of $0.9$ minus the last
    crossing of $0.1$ before it). The strict-route scripts (\path{collapse_test.py},
    \path{check_k2_saddle.py}) use the first upward crossing of $\frac12$ on the logged trajectory. No
    experiment of this section uses spectral events: bond spectra enter only through effective bond ranks
    (singular values above $0.1$ times the smallest teacher weight, at checkpoints and at the end) and, in
    the route test, through the second Schmidt value as the route amplitude.
  \item GD outcome classes, checked in this order (\path{analyze_gd.py}): \emph{diverged}; \emph{GF-like}: final
    loss at most $1.05\Loss_{\rm GF}(T)+10^{-3}\Loss_0$, with $\Loss_{\rm GF}(T)$ the reference at the same time, and no
    step raising the loss by more than $10^{-3}\Loss_0$; \emph{catapult}: the same final loss after such a
    rise; \emph{thrown back to the origin}: not at that level and the loss back above $0.9\Loss_0$ after the
    first event; \emph{bounded oscillation}: over the last $20\%$ of the steps (at most 2000) the total
    variation of the loss exceeds ten times its net change and the tail reaches above gradient flow's
    level, or the end point is linearly unstable ($\lr\lambda_{\max}/2>1.001$); \emph{other}: none of these (it
    did not occur in the runs shown). GF-like includes 39 of the 195 small-initialization runs that end,
    like their references, on the last plateau ($\Loss/\Loss_0=0.071$).
  \item SGD: the first escape is the first time any component reaches $\frac12$, compared with gradient
    flow's; a run \emph{forgot} if its first-learned component later falls below $\frac12$. Runs are
    classified before pooling; means $\pm$ s.e.\ are across initializations. Second plateaus use only
    initializations with a resolved gradient-flow plateau ($\sep1>3$) and no near-tie (the eventually
    second component does not peak between $0.15$ and $0.6$ during the first event), and only runs that
    kept the first component and gradient flow's order. Spread exponents: per initialization, least
    squares of $\ln{\rm sd}(\tev1)$ against $\ln(\lr/\batch)$. Collapse law: weighted least squares (weights
    $1/{\rm s.e.}^2$) of $T_{\rm SGD}/T_{\rm GF}$ over the 19 conditions in which every run escaped, one $c_\gamma$
    per law; \emph{trapped}: no persistent event by $4T_{\rm GF}$.
  \item Finite samples: test error $\normF{\W-\Wt}^2/\normF{\Wt}^2$; \emph{recovered}: test error below
    $10^{-2}$ (thresholds $10^{-3}$ and $10^{-1}$ give the same picture); effective bond rank: singular values
    above $0.2$, a tenth of the smallest teacher weight, averaged over the edges; the lazy limit depends
    only on the initial direction and is counted once per (setting, $\nsamp$, seed).
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Problems.}
Whitened Gaussian inputs, exact population loss (empirical loss in the finite-sample sweep), double
precision. Teacher seed 100; initialization seeds 0--5, from a stream independent of the teacher's.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{binary4}: binary tree, $\Ns=4$ ($\Kc=3$), $d=3$, $\chi=4$; odeco teacher with weights
    $(3,2,1)$ and Haar-random site bases.
  \item \emph{binary8}, \emph{MPS8}: $\Ns=8$ ($\Kc=7$ and $8$), the same odeco teacher, $d=3$, $\chi=4$.
  \item \emph{E8} on binary8 and MPS8: pattern teacher with $d=2$, $\chi=3$ and patterns

    $\pi_B$ equal to $2$ at sites 2 and 7 (weight $0.7$), $1$ elsewhere. The cores that carry the
    differing sites span $k=2$ ($\pi_A$) and $k=6$ ($\pi_B$) cores on the MPS and $k=5$ for both on
    the binary tree.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Initializations.}
Fan-in \eqref{eq:init} with per-core scale $\isc$; \emph{balanced}: the same $\Wz$ in the gauge with
$\Q e=0$ on every edge. All deep-tree baselines use the balanced version, because fan-in gradient flow
converges by $t=2\cdot10^6$ in none of the 12 MPS8 odeco runs (the conservation-law trap of
\cref{prop:A-trap}, verified at the route ends in 11 of the 12 stalls), against 10 of 12 (binary8
odeco), 11 of 12 (binary8 E8) and 11 of 12 (MPS8 E8) fan-in runs. Methods whose iterates depend on the
gauge were run from both versions; re-gauged GD, Riemannian GD, ALS and DMRG depend only on $\Wz$
and were run from the balanced one.

\paragraph{Dynamics.}
All methods stop at $\Loss<10^{-6}\Loss_0$ (finite samples: empirical loss below $10^{-8}$ times its
initial value) or at their budget.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{Gradient flow}: SciPy LSODA with relative and absolute tolerances $10^{-9}$ and
    $10^{-14}$ and the exact Hessian as Jacobian (Radau as fallback), instead of the default
    integrator of \cref{sec:setup-dynamics}; the crossings $\ov r\in\{0.1,0.5,0.9\}$ are located by
    root finding, so event times are exact. Budgets: $t_{\max}=8/\isc$ (binary4), $2\cdot10^6$
    (deep trees), $4\cdot10^6$ (binary8 scan), $10^6$ (finite samples).
  \item \emph{GD and heavy ball} (PyTorch): $\lr=0.02$ and $0.002$ with $\beta=0.9$ on binary4
    ($0.13\,\lr_c$ for both, counting heavy ball's effective step $\lr/(1-\beta)$); $\lr=0.01$ and
    $0.001$ on the deep trees ($0.23$--$0.27\,\lr_c$), at $\isc=0.8$ only, because gradient-flow
    plateaus at $\isc=0.3$ last $10^4$--$10^6$ time units. Budgets $8/\isc$ (binary4) and $2\cdot10^4$
    (deep) gradient-flow time units, at most $5\cdot10^5$ steps.
  \item \emph{Adam} (PyTorch, bias-corrected): $\beta_1=0.9$, $\beta_2=0.999$ (also $0.99$ and $0.9$
    at $\lr=10^{-3}$ on binary4), $\epsilon=10^{-8}$ ($10^{-30}$ in the $\epsilon$ sweep),
    $\lr\in\{10^{-4},3\cdot10^{-4},10^{-3},10^{-2}\}$ on binary4 and $\{10^{-3},10^{-2}\}$ on the deep
    trees. \emph{RMSProp}: $\alpha=0.99$, $\epsilon=10^{-8}$, $\lr\in\{10^{-4},10^{-3}\}$. Budgets
    $10^5$--$3\cdot10^5$ (binary4), $3\cdot10^5$ (deep), $5\cdot10^5$ (binary8 scan) and $2\cdot10^5$
    ($\epsilon$ sweep) steps.
  \item \emph{Re-gauged GD}: $\lr=0.02$ (binary4) and $0.01$ (deep); QR sweeps from the leaves to
    the root bring the cores to the root-centred canonical form every 1 or 50 steps; $5\cdot10^4$,
    $10^5$ and $2\cdot10^5$ (binary8 scan) steps.
  \item \emph{Riemannian GD}: $\lr\in\{0.1,1\}$; tangent projection by least squares with the dense
    Jacobian of $\W(\params)$ (relative cutoff $10^{-12}$), so only small $d^\Ns$ are feasible;
    leaves-to-root HSVD retraction; 2000 steps.
  \item \emph{Gram-preconditioned GD}: $\lr\in\{0.01,0.1,0.5\}$; each Kronecker factor of a core's
    Hessian block is damped by $10^{-3}$ times its mean eigenvalue (scale-free) or, at $\lr=0.1$, by
    an absolute floor $10^{-4}$ or $10^{-2}$; no divergence guard, because the first step overshoots
    by up to $10^{34}\Loss_0$ before the loss returns; $5\cdot10^4$ (binary4; $2\cdot10^5$ with a
    floor) and $10^5$ (deep) steps.
  \item \emph{ALS} (one site) and \emph{DMRG} (two sites, then SVD truncation to at most $\chi$
    values with relative cutoff $10^{-12}$): exact least squares; the orthogonality centre moves by QR
    along a depth-first tour of the tree; at most 50 sweeps.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Events and classification.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Event $r$ is the last upward crossing of $\ov r=\frac12$ after which $\ov r$ stays at least
    $\frac12$; transient crossings are not events (\cref{sec:setup-diagnostics}). Width: final rise
    from $0.1$ to $0.9$. Saddle-to-saddle: every
    separation ratio $\sep i>3$ (\cref{tab:regimes}).
  \item Discrete methods record loss and overlaps at every step for the first 2000 steps, then every
    $\max(1,n_{\rm steps}/(5\cdot10^4))$ steps. A first escape or gap shorter than two recording
    intervals is unresolved and excluded from fits (and counted in the result tables). Two components
    that cross $\frac12$ in the same step or micro-step have no resolved order. For Gram-preconditioned
    GD, events are defined only after the loss has returned below $\Loss_0$; a component already above
    $\frac12$ at that point has no resolved event.
  \item Exponents: per seed, a least-squares fit of $\ln T$ against $\ln\isc$ over the $\isc$ at which
    the seed follows its most common learning order (for plateau durations, not for times to reach a
    loss level); mean $\pm$ standard error over seeds. A logarithmic law fitted in the same space
    serves as comparison (gradient flow on binary4: root-mean-square residual of $\ln T$ $0.005$ for
    the power law, $0.62$ for the logarithmic law).
  \item Orders are compared with gradient flow only where both are resolved, for the same problem,
    $\isc$ and seed. Re-gauged GD, Riemannian GD, ALS and DMRG give one trajectory per seed at every
    $\isc$, so their orders are counted over the six independent seeds (at the smallest $\isc$).
  \item \emph{$\epsilon$ test} \eqref{eq:adam-eps}: $T_{\rm GF}(\isc)$ is the seed's exact
    gradient-flow first-escape time at $\isc=0.3$ (\texttt{deep} sweep, same balanced initial point
    up to scale) times $(0.3/\isc)^6$, and $n_{\rm sign}$ the median over $\isc$ of the same seed's
    escape with $\epsilon=10^{-30}$ at the same $\lr$. Runs without an escape within $2\cdot10^5$ steps
    enter only through the check that the prediction exceeds the budget.
  \item Convergence for cost and time-to-loss tables: $\Loss<10^{-3}\Loss_0$. Runs that hit their budget
    are reported as budget-limited, never as stalls. RMSProp hovers above $10^{-6}\Loss_0$ on the deep
    trees within $3\cdot10^5$ steps; its events are used, its convergence flags are not.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Real data}\label{app:exp-real-data}
Settings of \cref{sec:real-data}. Experiment folder \path{experiments/real_data/} (README with every command
in order; YAML configurations in \path{configs/}); findings in \path{notes/real_data.md}, whose section 8
(fix round) supersedes its sections 4--7 and whose section 9 holds the pre-registered order test; review
in \path{notes/reviews/real_data_round1.md}.

\paragraph{Data and model.}
MNIST (torchvision mirror) and Fashion-MNIST, fetched and downsampled by \path{download.py}: images cropped
to the central $24\times24$ pixels and block-averaged to $4\times4$ ($6\times6$ blocks) or $8\times8$ ($3\times3$ blocks),
intensities in $[0,1]$. Tasks: MNIST 3 ($y=+1$) against 5 ($y=-1$); Fashion-MNIST sneaker (7) against ankle
boot (9), chosen before any run for a visible shape difference at $4\times4$. Balanced subsamples of 4000
training and 1600 test images, data seed 0. Site features $\feat{}(x)=(\cos\frac{\pi x}2,\sin\frac{\pi x}2)$
(\emph{raw}), or per site $A_i\feat{}$ with $A_i\E[\feat{}\feat{}\T]A_i\T=\Id$ (\emph{white}; uncentred second moment,
eigenvalues floored at $10^{-3}$ of the largest), which keeps the function class. Model
$f(x)=\ip{\W}{\bigotimes_i\feat{}(x_i)}$ and loss $\frac1{2\nsamp}\sum_j(f(x_j)-y_j)^2$; in the regime sweep the
centred model trains $f-f_0$. Trees from \path{data.make_tree}: binary16 ($\Kc=15$) and MPS16 ($\Kc=16$),
binary64 ($\Kc=63$) and MPS64 ($\Kc=64$) for $8\times8$ images; $\chi=4$ ($8$ in one sweep, where the bottom bonds
are capped at 4 by their two-pixel cut). Pixel orders (\path{data.pixel_order}; site $s$ carries pixel
$\mathrm{order}[s]$, pixels numbered row by row): quadtree (Morton key, column bit lowest), snake
(boustrophedon rows), scrambled$k$ (random permutation from \path{numpy.random.default_rng}$(k+12345)$),
and for the order test quadtreeT (Morton order of the transposed image), rowmajor, colmajor, hilbert
(Hilbert curve) and mortonrev (site $s$ carries the pixel whose Morton key is $s$ with its bits
reversed). Fan-in initialization \eqref{eq:init} at per-core scale $\isc$ with initialization seeds
0--4, independent of the data seed. Double precision, one thread per process.

\paragraph{Dynamics} (\path{run.py}).
Phase 1: Dormand--Prince 5(4) with relative and absolute tolerances $10^{-8}$ and $10^{-14}$, the clock
re-anchored ($t\to0$, offset kept) whenever an accepted step is shorter than $10^{-9}t$, so that escapes at
$t\sim10^{15}$--$10^{20}$ are resolved; it stops at the first accepted step at which the loss has fallen
by $1\%$ of $\Loss(0)$ (time $t_a$). Phase 2: LSODA with the same tolerances on the clock $\tau=t-t_a$, with the
Gauss--Newton part of the Hessian (closed-form per-sample Jacobians) as Newton matrix, up to
$\tau=10^4$ ($3000$ for $8\times8$ images; $\min(3000,10^4$ kernel units$)$ in the regime sweep, a kernel unit
being $128/\tr\TK$ at $t_a$). Records: 30 logarithmic times per decade, 1000 linear times (600 for $8\times8$)
and the library's fine rows, merged for every event time. Conservation drift (cumulative, relative to
the largest squared core norm) at most $4.6\cdot10^{-8}$, median $1.8\cdot10^{-9}$ over the 234 main runs.
Two cases rerun with tolerances $10^{-10}$ and $10^{-16}$ (\texttt{tol\_check}) agree in $t_a$ to 7 digits, in
the final loss to 6, and in later drop times to $0.1\%$; the first drop ($\tau\approx10$--$15$) moves by
$0.2$--$0.8$ time units, below every gap reported.

\paragraph{Events and analyses.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{Loss drops} (\path{analysis.py}): peaks of $-\dd\Loss/\dd t$ that stand out by a factor 3
    against the minima separating them from higher peaks and lower the loss by at least $1\%$ of
    $\Loss(0)$; time: the peak; width: its full width at half maximum. Widths by progress (from $10\%$ to
    $90\%$ of a drop) are not used, since the loss creeps between drops and such widths measure the
    creep. Separation ratios and the S2S signature as in \cref{sec:setup-diagnostics}, with these drops.
  \item \emph{Data-weighted bond spectra} (\path{data.function_spectra}): across edge $e$ the network
    computes $f(x)=\sum_au_a(x)v_a(x)$, with messages $u_a$ from below the edge and $v_a$ from above it; the
    Schmidt values are the square roots of the eigenvalues of $G_u^{1/2}G_vG_u^{1/2}$, where $G_u$ and $G_v$
    are the Gram matrices of the two messages on the training set. They are gauge invariant and equal
    the Frobenius spectra under a product law with identity second moments (checked to
    $2\cdot10^{-12}$). Frobenius spectra are not used: after the escape $\normF{\W}$ grows to
    $10^6$--$10^7$ along directions the data barely see.
  \item \emph{Rank events}: $\bs ek/\bs e1$ crossing $0.05$ (log-interpolated); an edge belongs to a drop if
    it crosses inside the drop's half-maximum window widened by one width on each side. Crossings already
    met at $\tau=0$ are flagged as unresolved.
  \item \emph{First-route gaps} (\path{scaling.py}): gap $=$ drop 1 $-$ drop 0 on the $\tau$ clock; a route's
    drop is the one whose widened window contains its rank crossing, and points where it rose between
    drops, merged with drop 0, or where another route rose first are excluded with their reason. Clean
    series: one (order, seed) whose first route, one sibling pair, rises inside drop 1 at every $\isc$, with at
    least four $\isc$ over at least half a decade (11 series); full sweep: 91 of the 124 binary points
    kept (16 between drops, 2 merged, 15 another route first), 10 seed series. Fits per seed: power law
    by least squares in $\ln g$, log law and $A/\isc+B$ by weighted least squares with weights $1/g$; all
    compared by the relative rms residual.
  \item \emph{MPS edges}: time of each edge's first rise after the first drop, per seed over the seven
    $\isc$, both laws fitted with relative residuals (\path{scaling.py}, \texttt{edge\_scaling}).
  \item \emph{Route amplitudes} (\path{route_amplitudes.py}). A binary TTN of bond rank one computes
    $f=c\prod_vq_v$, $q_v=g_v\cdot\Feat_v$, over its bottom cores $v$, $\Feat_v$ the features of the two pixels
    of $v$. For a pair $(u,w)$ of bottom cores, with the residual $r_j=f(x_j)-y_j$ and $h_{uw}=c\prod_{v\ne u,w}q_v$,
    \begin{equation}\label{eq:real-route-amplitude}
      \mu_{uw}=\norm{P_uM_{uw}P_w}_2,\qquad
      M_{uw}=\frac1\nsamp\sum_jr_j\,h_{uw}(x_j)\,\Feat_u(x_j)\Feat_w(x_j)\T,
    \end{equation}
    with $P_u=\Id-g_ug_u\T/\norm{g_u}^2$; $k_{uw}$ is the number of cores of the tree path from $u$ to $w$.
    Evaluated for all 28 pairs at the plateau midpoint (minimum of $\norm{\dot\params}$ between the first
    two drops, reached by re-integrating from the end of phase 1). States farther than $0.05$ (relative) from a rank-one product are
    dropped (28 of 124). Units (\path{analysis.route_units}): rank-one states of the same (order, seed)
    whose normalized patterns $\mu_{uw}^2/\sum\mu^2$ differ by less than $0.2$ in total variation and whose
    plateau losses differ by less than $0.01$; 96 states form 26 units in the 20 (order, seed) pairs.
  \item \emph{Hessians} (\path{plateau_hessians.py}): exact Hessian (dense residual tensor) at the
    plateau midpoint (minimum $\norm{\dot\params}$ between the first two drops), raw and gauge-projected
    (\cref{sec:setup-diagnostics}); exponents per seed over the runs at the common plateau level
    ($\Loss\in[0.265,0.275]$ binary, $[0.225,0.235]$ MPS; $[0.31,0.345]$ on Fashion-MNIST, pooled).
  \item \emph{Regime sweep} (\path{regime_analysis.py}): kernel on the first 128 test images; labels by
    \cref{tab:regimes} without the convergence rule, which no $\chi=4$ network meets on 4000 labels (lazy if
    $\dTK(T)<0.1$; S2S if at least two drops, all $\sep{}>3$; rich otherwise); the linearized model at
    $\params(t_a)$ in closed form, with its floor $\Loss_{\rm lin}(\infty)$.
  \item \emph{Order test} (\path{order_predictor.py}, \path{order_test.py}; registered analysis): the
    best rank-one fit $f=\prod_vg_v\cdot\Feat_v$ of the labels from 32 starts (start $i$: unit $g_v$ from
    \path{default_rng}$(1000+i)$, 50 steps of higher-order power iteration on $\frac1\nsamp\sum_jy_j\bigotimes_v\Feat_v(x_j)$,
    rescaling to the least-squares amplitude, then alternating least squares until the relative loss
    change is below $10^{-12}$ or 2000 sweeps), the lowest-loss fit balanced, $S$ from
    \eqref{eq:real-route-amplitude} at that state; outcomes O1 (mean over seeds of $\Loss(10^4)$) and O2
    (mean of $\log_{10}\tau(\Loss\le0.25)$ on the running minimum, censored at $10^4$); Spearman $\rho$ over
    orders, percentile bootstrap intervals (10,000 resamples of orders) and two-sided permutation
    $p$-values (10,000 permutations of $S$).
\end{itemize}
