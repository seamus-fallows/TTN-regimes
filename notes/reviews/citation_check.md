# Citation audit: report and literature note (cite-check, 2026-10-10)

Scope: all 129 entries of `report/references.bib`; all 103 `\cite` commands (68 distinct keys) in
`report/sections/*.tex`; every reference and pointer in `notes/literature.md` v1.1 (213 references).
Method: metadata against arXiv abstract pages (versions and journal-ref), proceedings / publisher pages and
Crossref; pointers (theorem, lemma, equation numbers, constants, exponents, quotes) read in the arXiv HTML
or in the PDF pages themselves. The version read is given in each row ("v2" = arXiv version 2). Lead-level
papers were read directly (Kumar & Haupt x4, Furman et al., Rawal & DeWeese, Achour et al., Zhang-Saxe-Latham,
Kunin et al. AGF, Razin et al. 2022, Jiang-Chen-Ding, Saxe et al. 2014); the remaining clusters were read by
eight sub-checks with the same rules, and prior work by two search passes (section b).

Severity: **wrong** (the source does not say this, or the metadata is false), **imprecise** (right idea,
wrong scope, assumptions, pointer or attribution), **ok-but-improve** (correct, but a sharper pointer or
qualifier helps the reader). Rows marked "fixed" were corrected on this branch (`references.bib`,
`notes/literature.md`); all report rows are for the lead to apply (report sections were not edited).

## Summary: what the lead must know

1. **Five "new" claims have prior or concurrent work** (section b): the closed-form Adam law with
   epsilon (05:110-117, `n_1 ~ (eps/eta) T_GF + n_sign`) is Prop. 7 of Sabanayagam & Lucey (arXiv
   2610.04249, 3 Oct 2026, aligned balanced deep matrix factorization); the EoS period-two amplitude
   (A:1237-1245) follows from MacDonald et al. (NeurIPS 2025), Thm 5.3 with the cubic coefficient of their
   Prop. B.3, eq. 54 (same formula after rescaling; they do not write it out); "KKT points of g are exactly
   balanced" (prop:A-kkt, also in CLAUDE.md) is Lemma 5 of Kumar & Haupt (TMLR 2025) for linear chains and
   follows in one line from the proof of Furman et al. Prop. F.10; the per-initialization blow-up identity
   (cor:A-blowup) is AGF's activation law (Kunin et al. 2025, Eqs. 2-3); the k >= 3 head start uses AGF's
   carried dormant norms (the closed form is new). Cite them and narrow the `\newresult` tags (a1 rows 11,
   15, 16, 40, 53).
2. **Furman et al. 2026 is closer to the route rule than the report says.** Their Fig. 4 caption: with
   several disjoint rank-deficient cores "each core represents one escape path in the saddle point, and
   the smallest core delivers the least suppressed escape (Remark D.10)", and Remark D.10 already links the
   order N to the leap complexity of Abbe et al. What remains new is that the route can be a proper subtree
   (the Steiner subtree of the residual's sites) inside one connected rank-deficient component, and the
   dynamics. They never state that N = 2 gives a strict saddle (04:106-108, A:613-614 attribute it).
3. **Three wrong pointers in the report, two outdated bib entries**: Razin et al. 2022 is not our model
   (CP-structured nodes, 02:81-83; this resolves the TODO at 02:84); `hackbusch2009new` does not contain the
   cross-tree rank bound (A:1029); the sign-flow limit of Adam is Ma, Wu & E 2022, not Balles & Hennig
   (05:84-85). `kumar2024grokking` and `zunkovic2022grokking` were listed as preprints but are ICLR 2024 and
   JMLR 2024 (fixed in the bib).
4. **Scope qualifiers missing at the anchor citations**: Achour et al. state non-strictness for exactly
   0-balanced init and saddles with S = [1, r] (04:108-110); Kumar & Haupt's Thm 8 is a lower bound on the
   plateau with a Lojasiewicz local minimum (04:101-104, A:523); Jacot et al. never call the saddles SVD
   truncations (A:689-690) and their probability-one case L = 3 assumes a full-rank gradient (A:419-421);
   Du, Hu & Lee have the discrete identity only for two-layer factorization (A:89).
5. **Version numbering**: Wesel & Batselier (Cor B.6 in PMLR, B.8 in arXiv v1), Uschmajew & Vandereycken
   (report numbers vs LAA numbers), Kumar & Haupt JMLR (Thm 11 in every version, not "Thm 10 earlier").
6. Corlouer et al.'s printed Ito drift (Prop 3.5, eq. 6) contradicts the report's "noise cannot drive the
   first escape" and is not covariant under SGD's rescaling symmetry; two independent checks here find it
   suspect, so cite their experiments, not that formula (A:1274-1276).
7. 61 of the 129 original bib entries are cited only in LaTeX comments or not at all and do not appear in
   the printed bibliography (no `\nocite`); intended or not, the lead should decide.
