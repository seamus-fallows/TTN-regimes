# optimizers: does the saddle-to-saddle picture survive Adam, preconditioned/Riemannian GD and ALS/DMRG?

Status: done (fix round after review round 1) - Branch: agent/optimizers - Updated: 2026-10-09 21:45 UTC

## Summary
Asked (backlog B1): which features of the gradient-flow (GF) saddle-to-saddle (S2S) picture of TTN training
(plateaus, one-at-a-time learning, learning order, plateau ~ sigma^-(K-2)) survive the optimizers used in
practice. Tested on binary N=4 (K=3), binary N=8 (K=7) and MPS N=8 (K=8), odeco (3, 2, 1) and the E8 locality
teacher, 6 seeds per setting.

**GD and momentum reproduce GF.** Their shifts follow P6: GD's 0.0199 per e-fold of 1/sigma against 0.020
predicted; heavy ball's is (1 + beta) / (1 - beta) times larger.

**Adam / RMSProp keep GF's selection of the first component (94-100%), but not its time scales:**
- the first escape does not depend on the init scale (p = 0.01 on binary4 over 1.8 decades, 0.05 / 0.15 on
  binary8, against GF's 1.007 and 4.99);
- later plateaus still grow as the init shrinks on binary8, but no law is established;
- with the default eps, below a crossover sigma_x Adam inherits GF's sigma^-(K-2) plateau with prefactor
  eps / lr. On MPS8 a parameter-free prediction of this holds per seed to 0.1%.

**ALS/DMRG and Riemannian GD have no plateaus and no init-scale dependence.** ALS/DMRG fit all components
within the same micro-steps (no resolved order); Riemannian GD stages them by weight on binary8.

**Re-gauged GD** is sigma-free but sequential on deep trees.

**Gram-preconditioned GD** is not sigma-free (withdrawn from that group).

**Fix round:** the review's points 1-7 are all addressed (claims changed: `notes/optimizers.md` section 5).

## Decisions
- **Fix round: superseded runs moved, not deleted** (`/root/outputs/optimizers/<sweep>/superseded/`): GF runs with
  grid-interpolated events, Gram runs stopped by the loss guard, binary8 Adam runs with the 10-step trace.
- **Saddle-start protocol not run**: the review offered it as the way to keep the "half" claim; I withdrew the
  claim instead, because a fresh Adam state at a saddle has no second-moment memory and would not reproduce the
  later plateaus (the protocol needs the warm state; design in `notes/optimizers.md` section 4).
- **Finite samples redone** (rather than withdrawn only): 4 data draws x 6 inits, paired comparisons.
- **Balanced init for deep-tree GF baselines** (options: fan-in only, chi = d, gauge-balanced version of the same
  W_0). Fan-in GF stalls on MPS8 / binary8 (dormant components die, `notes/theory_saddles.md` R12) and chi = 3
  stalls too; balancing keeps W_0 and gives clean S2S. Fan-in kept as the practitioners' default for the
  gauge-dependent methods.
- **Clocks**: GF time for GF / GD / heavy ball; steps for Adam-type, preconditioned and Riemannian methods; sweeps
  for ALS / DMRG. Exponents are clock-independent.
- **GD / heavy ball on deep trees only at sigma = 0.8** (at 0.3 GF plateaus last 1e4-1e6 time units).
- **eps sweep budget 2e5 steps** (first escape is the measurement; later escapes at small sigma are out of
  reach); budget-limited runs are reported as such and used in the prediction test.
- **Riemannian GD implemented densely** (exact, simple, not scalable beyond d^N ~ 1e4).
- **No divergence guard for Gram preconditioning** (the overshoot is the method's behaviour).
- **Adaptive recording** (full diagnostics when the running-minimum loss or running-maximum overlap moves) to
  resolve events whatever the step budget; rate-limited after it slowed oscillating Adam runs.
- **Results directory**: analysis and figure scripts write outside git by default; the committed results
  were regenerated from scratch with `--results-dir experiments/optimizers/results` after the analysis
  standards arrived (old summary CSVs deleted and rebuilt: my own outputs).
- **Not done**: CLAUDE.md left to the lead (my findings are in `notes/optimizers.md`). Twice I stopped my own
  sweeps by matching their command lines (once with `pkill -f experiments.optimizers.run`, before the new
  "kill only PIDs" rule was on main); on this machine they matched only my processes.

## Caveats and next steps
- Sizes are small (N <= 8, d <= 3, chi <= 4) and teachers orthogonal (odeco, one pattern teacher); claims are for
  these settings. Deep-tree sigma ranges span 0.5 decade (exponents indicative); only lr <= 0.27 eta_c tested
  for GD / heavy ball.
- Adam's later plateaus have no established law; the saddle-start protocol with Adam's warm state
  (`notes/optimizers.md` section 4) is the next step if this matters for the report.
- ALS / DMRG and Riemannian GD at lr 1 on binary4 learn within one or two steps / micro-steps: no resolved order;
  only their sigma-independence and cost are measured.
- RMSProp never reaches L < 1e-6 L0 on deep trees within 3e5 steps (it hovers); its events are fine, its
  convergence flags are not.
- The heavy-ball (1 + beta) / (1 - beta) factor is a fitted observation (one beta, one lr), not derived.
- P6 path form (needs |grad L| at the exact event) not evaluated; the asymptotic slope is confirmed.
- Re-gauged GD on MPS8 odeco: budget-limited at 1e3 GF time units (stall or slow escape undecided).
- Suggested library additions: gauge balancing (`gauge.balance`) and canonical forms (`gauge.canonicalize`)
  (tests in `experiments/optimizers/test_optimizers.py`).
