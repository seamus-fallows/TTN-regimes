# optimizers: does the saddle-to-saddle picture survive Adam, preconditioned/Riemannian GD and ALS/DMRG?

Status: done - Branch: agent/optimizers - Updated: 2026-10-09 20:45 UTC

## Summary
Asked (backlog B1): which features of the gradient-flow (GF) saddle-to-saddle (S2S) picture of TTN training
(plateaus, one-at-a-time rank growth, learning order, plateau ~ sigma^-(K-2)) survive the optimizers used in
practice. Tested on binary N=4 (K=3), binary N=8 (K=7) and MPS N=8 (K=8), with odeco (3, 2, 1) and the E8
locality teacher, 6 seeds per setting, ~2400 runs.

**GD and heavy ball reproduce GF quantitatively.**

**Adam / RMSProp keep the sequential learning and GF's learning order, but not GF's time scales:**
- the first escape no longer depends on the init scale (exponent 0.01 +- 0.01 vs GF 1.007 +- 0.001 on
  binary4; 0.05 +- 0.02 vs 4.76 +- 0.06 on binary8);
- later plateaus are set by Adam's second-moment memory and its adaptive edge of stability, and on binary8
  they grow with 1/sigma at about half GF's rate;
- below a crossover sigma_eps (~0.1 per core on K = 7-8 with the default eps = 1e-8), Adam is GD in
  disguise and inherits GF's sigma^-(K-2) plateau, as a parameter-free prediction confirms per seed to
  0.97-1.05.

**Methods that act on the tensor or fix the gauge remove the plateaus altogether:** Riemannian GD, ALS/DMRG
and Gram-preconditioned GD learn all components at once, in a sigma-independent time (exactly for ALS/DMRG,
proven), in an order unrelated to the teacher weights. GD with re-gauging is plateau-free on binary4 but
sequential (sigma-free) and stall-prone on deep trees.

### Theory (notes/optimizers.md section 1)
- Proven: invariance table of all methods; ALS / DMRG iterates are independent of sigma.
- Derived and confirmed:
  - Adam forgets the init scale (sign-flow limit lr n_1 -> const);
  - the eps crossover n_1 = (eps / lr) T_GF(sigma) + n_sign (to 0.97-1.05 per seed);
  - re-gauged GD has no sigma dependence;
  - the block-diagonal Gauss-Newton overshoot grows as sigma^-K(K-1).
- Wrong or corrected:
  - an absolute damping floor in Gram preconditioning does not bring the plateau back in the tested sigma
    range (it acts once per bond leg);
  - re-gauged GD is not plateau-free on deep trees (random frames capture only ~(chi / d^n)^2 of the
    teacher);
  - the scalar-model return time after the preconditioner overshoot is 3x too short.
- Heuristic, partly supported: Adam's later plateaus = memory phase, then adaptive edge of stability.

## Decisions
- **Balanced init for deep-tree GF baselines** (options: fan-in only, chi = d, gauge-balanced version of the same
  W_0). Fan-in GF stalls on MPS8 / binary8 (dormant components die, `notes/theory_saddles.md` R12) and chi = 3
  stalls too; balancing keeps W_0 and gives clean S2S. Fan-in kept as the practitioners' default for the
  gauge-dependent methods.
- **Clocks**: GF time for GF / GD / heavy ball; steps for Adam-type, preconditioned and Riemannian methods; sweeps
  for ALS / DMRG. Exponents are clock-independent.
- **GD / heavy ball on deep trees only at sigma = 0.8** (at 0.3 GF plateaus last 1e4-1e6 time units).
- **eps sweep budget 2e5 steps** (first escape is the measurement; later escapes at small sigma are out of
  reach); budget-limited runs are reported as such and used in the prediction test.
- **Riemannian GD implemented densely** (exact, simple, not scalable beyond d^N ~ 1e4).
- **No divergence guard for Gram preconditioning** (the overshoot is the method's behaviour).
- **Adaptive recording** (full diagnostics when the running-minimum loss or running-maximum overlap moves) to
  resolve events whatever the step budget; rate-limited after it slowed oscillating Adam runs.
- **Results directory**: analysis and figure scripts write outside git by default; the committed results
  were regenerated from scratch with `--results-dir experiments/optimizers/results` after the analysis
  standards arrived (old summary CSVs deleted and rebuilt: my own outputs).
- **Not done**: CLAUDE.md left to the lead (my findings are in `notes/optimizers.md`). Twice I stopped my own
  sweeps by matching their command lines (once with `pkill -f experiments.optimizers.run`, before the new
  "kill only PIDs" rule was on main); on this machine they matched only my processes.

## Caveats and next steps
- Sizes are small (N <= 8, d <= 3, chi <= 4) and teachers orthogonal (odeco, one pattern teacher); claims are for
  these settings. Deep-tree exponents span 0.5 decade of sigma only (indicative).
- Adam's later-plateau exponent (~half GF's on binary8) is measured, not explained; a saddle-start protocol
  (controlled dormant amplitude at a learned saddle) would separate Adam's memory from the dormant amplitude.
- Riemannian GD and ALS / DMRG events happen within 1-2 steps: their event times are unresolved by design; only
  their sigma-independence and cost are measured.
- RMSProp never reaches L < 1e-6 L0 on deep trees within 3e5 steps (it hovers); its events are fine, its
  convergence flags are not.
- P6 (GD event shift) was only checked in magnitude; a dense-output GF rerun near events would make it precise
  (optim-samples' scope).
- Suggested library additions: gauge balancing (`gauge.balance`) and canonical forms (`gauge.canonicalize`) are
  generally useful (tests in `experiments/optimizers/test_optimizers.py`).
